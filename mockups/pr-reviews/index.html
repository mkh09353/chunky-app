Chunky

PR Reviews — layout mockups

Three static explorations of a GitHub PR review surface: open PRs you authored, plus PRs from others waiting on your review, with Chunky sessions linked to both.

Static HTML and CSS. Same data and same card vocabulary in all three, so only the layout differs.

1

Sidebar rail + slide-over

→

Ambient by default: a compact summary widget pinned in the app sidebar keeps the counts in peripheral vision, and clicking a row slides a detail panel over the main content. Cheapest in screen real estate, one click away from the full lists.

always-visible counts slide-over panel zero layout cost
2

Dedicated PR board

→

A top-level tab beside sessions and zoo. Two columns, Mine and Ready for Review, with the org selector and refresh timestamp in the page header. The most room per card and the best triage view, but it is a place you have to navigate to.

two columns org dropdown + refresh roomiest cards
3

Third pane

→

A persistent 340px column to the right of the transcript, My PRs stacked over Ready for Review, with unread dots for new activity. Always present while you work, at the cost of permanent horizontal space and the tightest card density.

unread activity dots persistent alongside chat compact cards

Shared conventions

  • Status dots: CI passing, CI failing, CI pending, informational or unread.
  • Headline stat on your own PRs is the unresolved comment count; a Resolve Comments action appears whenever it is above zero.
  • Ready-for-review cards sort oldest-waiting first and carry an age badge, amber past a day and red past three.
  • Linked sessions render as a pulsing strip in the accent colour on the card they belong to.
  • Tokens are lifted from src/mainview/index.css (dark theme); DM Sans for UI, JetBrains Mono for repo and branch names.
