variant 4

Centered session title

The empty space names what you're looking at: the current session title, muted, 12px, one line, truncating at ~34ch, with a small --primary dot when the session is running. Tabs, branch pill and the right cluster are untouched, and the title layer is click-through apart from the label itself, so the strip stays draggable. The rule is macOS document-window centering — absolutely centered on the window, not on the leftover space — unless the tabs would overlap it, in which case the title yields into the remaining gap. Worth knowing before choosing this: with the 288px sidebar and seven tabs, the tab row already runs past window centre (purple hairline), so the fallback is the normal state, not the edge case.
